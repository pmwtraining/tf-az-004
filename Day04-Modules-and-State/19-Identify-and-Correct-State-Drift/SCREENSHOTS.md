## Screenshot standards

- Use genuine screenshots from the student environment.
- Ensure commands and output are readable.
- Redact credentials, tokens and secrets.
- Include Bash and PowerShell evidence where applicable.
- Remove checklist items that are not relevant to this lab.
- Embed screenshots in the relevant sections of the lab instructions.
